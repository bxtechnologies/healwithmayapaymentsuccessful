How will I receive the ebook?

Straight after checkout you get an instant PDF download that works on your phone, tablet or computer.

Is this book only for fresh breakups?

No. Whether it ended last week or last year, the guide meets you where you are and moves at your pace.

I’m not a big reader. Is it long?

It’s a 19-page guide written in short, warm paragraphs. Read it in one evening, or one chapter a day alongside the 7-day challenge.

Will it tell me to stop feeling?

Never. The book says you are human and your feelings matter. It helps you stop letting every feeling make your decisions.

Is it a replacement for professional support?

No. It’s a gentle companion. If your pain feels very heavy, please also reach out to someone you trust or a qualified professional.
